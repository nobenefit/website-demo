Who We Are

Born Local.
Built to Last.

LOCAL was founded on a simple belief: where you come from shapes who you are. We started with a vision to create streetwear that felt real — no gimmicks, no excess, just clean design and quality craft.

Every collection we release carries a postcode — a tribute to the cities and communities that raised us. From 8301 Kimberley to 6835 George to CPT Cape Town, our pieces are a love letter to where you belong.

We believe fashion should empower, not define. Our tracksuits are made for real people living real lives — heavyweight fleece, oversized fits, and colourways that speak for themselves.
